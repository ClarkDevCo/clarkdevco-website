About

Two brothers building a company meant to last.

Chris and Alex Clark founded Clark Development Co. to buy, build and hold neighborhood retail and infill apartments. Chris brings nearly twenty years in real estate; Alex brings the underwriting.

Chris and Alex

Chris Clark

Co-Founder

Chris has spent nearly twenty years in real estate. He began in corporate aviation, flying for Foursquare Properties, moved into development in 2005, and later worked at Boulder Ventures Development in retail leasing and acquisitions. After 2008 he sold REOs as an asset manager, and since 2014 he has acquired more than 2,000 acres of land. He lives in Carlsbad, California.

Alex Clark

Co-Founder

Alex comes from private equity and financial engineering. He builds the models that tell us what a property can really earn, and what we should and shouldn’t pay for it.
