A buyer who reads your leases before we call.

Clark Development buys and repositions neighborhood strip centers in the Salt Lake Valley. Long-term, direct, and built around the tenants who make a center work.

Talk to us →

You built a center that people use every week. Maybe you’ve held it for decades. Maybe a tenant is leaving, a loan is coming due, or you’re simply not sure what to do next. Before you list it, or answer an unsolicited offer, talk with a buyer who understands the tenant side of retail.

We are not a fund looking for a spreadsheet. We are building a long-term retail portfolio, starting in Salt Lake. We plan to hold what we buy. That changes how we look at your property: not “how fast can this trade,” but “what does this center need to be great in ten years?”

What we’re looking for

Property
Neighborhood and strip centers, including centers with a pad or two, in the Salt Lake Valley. Typically valued at roughly $1 million to $7 million.
Tenants
Everyday-needs retail: grocery, pharmacy, restaurants, clinics, salons, services, local operators.
Situation
Well-leased centers; centers with vacancy to fill; deferred maintenance; centers with unused pad or parking land; owners looking for a clean exit or a partner.
What we pass on
Enclosed malls, single-tenant net lease, office.

Why owners talk to us

Relationships that last.

We plan to own for the long term, and we’d like to work with the same owners, brokers and tenants for years.

We know tenants.

Our background is in retail leasing and acquisitions. We read a rent roll the way a tenant’s broker would: who’s paying, who’s struggling, who’s below market.

We look at what the center could be.

Re-tenanting, a pad, a better use of parking, curb appeal. We’d rather tell you what we see than low-ball you in silence.

Flexible on structure.

A straight sale, or a partnership in which you keep an ownership stake.

A straight answer, fast.

We’ll tell you whether we’re interested. A fast “no” is also a service.

Discreet.

We keep your conversation confidential until you decide what to do.

How it works

  1. A conversation.

    A call with us. No pitch deck. You tell us about the center and what you’d like to happen.

  2. We review the numbers.

    You share the rent roll, leases and operating statements, under a confidentiality agreement if you like. We visit the center.

  3. A written proposal.

    We tell you in plain terms what we’d offer and under what conditions.

  4. Diligence and closing, with your tenants in mind.

    We work with your attorney and broker, if you have one. We aim for a transition your tenants barely notice.

What we bring

Experience across retail leasing, development and land; disciplined underwriting; and a long-term view.
