A PLACE WORTH BUILDING FOR

So much to love.
Too few places
to call home.

The coastline. The neighborhoods. The possibility of a life well lived.

San Diego is an extraordinary place. But for too many people, making a home here has become out of reach. There aren’t enough homes people can afford, and high housing costs put pressure on the people who make this region what it is.

As San Diego evolves, we see opportunity within the neighborhoods already here. Infill development puts vacant land and underused properties to better use—creating homes, reimagining buildings, and making room for the region’s next chapter.

More doors. More possibility. More San Diego.

GOOD DEVELOPMENT IS A TEAM EFFORT

Different expertise.
Shared possibility.

We’re building relationships with the people who can help turn underused San Diego properties into places that serve their neighborhoods.

01 Property owners +

Your property may have a bigger next chapter. We’re interested in vacant land, existing buildings, and underused properties with infill potential and straightforward conversations about your goals, timing, and options.

02 Realtors & brokers +

Have an on-market or off-market opportunity? Bring us the location, asking price, site details, and what you see in it. We value local knowledge and clear communication.

03 General contractors +

Great execution begins before breaking ground. We want to connect with contractors who bring practical cost guidance, constructability insight, and a collaborative approach to new construction, redevelopment, and building reuse.

04 Architects & consultants +

Help shape places that make sense for the people who use them, the site, and the budget. We welcome architects, designers, and planning specialists who balance good design with a clear path to delivery.

05 Engineers +

Sound engineering turns a promising concept into a buildable project. We’re looking to connect with civil, structural, geotechnical, and MEP engineers who bring practical solutions to site constraints, infrastructure, building systems, and construction coordination.

06 City, County & community +

Our aim is constructive collaboration with City of San Diego and County of San Diego teams, other local jurisdictions, and neighborhood stakeholders. We approach each site with respect for planning requirements, infrastructure, existing residents, and the community around it.

07 Investors +

We welcome conversations with investors who share our long-term view of San Diego and its need for more housing. Our approach starts with disciplined underwriting, thoughtful project selection, and alignment around the business plan, risks, and expectations. Let’s explore whether there’s a fit for a future infill development opportunity.
